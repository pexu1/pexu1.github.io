Physical oceanography

Current and previous research topics in physical oceanography.

Current affiliation

Atmospheric and Oceanic Science Group, Department of Earth and Planetary Science, The University of Tokyo.

Keywords

Physical oceanography Tropical instability waves Chlorophyll ENSO LLCEs MATLAB Python

Bio-physical coupling of TIW-scale chlorophyll variability

My PhD research focuses on quantifying bio-physical coupling of tropical instability wave-scale chlorophyll variability and its impact on ENSO.

Mixed layer depth, chlorophyll, and shortwave heating

I am interested in how mixed layer depth and chlorophyll associated with tropical instability waves modulate upper-ocean shortwave heating.

Long-lived cyclonic eddies off Western Australia

My undergraduate and master's research focused on long-lived cyclonic eddies off Western Australia, including statistics and energy analysis in the context of the Leeuwin Current system.

Observation and cruise experience

I have shipboard observation experience from research cruises, including work with glider, LADCP, AFP07, VMP2000, UVMP, and XCTD systems during KH-25-1 on R/V HAKUHO MARU.
