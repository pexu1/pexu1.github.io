Research

My work is in physical oceanography, with current emphasis on tropical instability waves, chlorophyll variability, mixed layer processes, and ENSO-related upper-ocean variability.

TIW-scale chlorophyll variability

Quantifying bio-physical coupling associated with tropical instability waves and its influence on ENSO.

Mixed layer depth and heating

Examining how mixed layer depth and chlorophyll modulate upper-ocean shortwave heating.

Long-lived cyclonic eddies

Previous undergraduate and master's work on LLCEs off Western Australia in the Leeuwin Current system.
